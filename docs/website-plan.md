# EzyPrint Website Plan

*Draft 3, 11 Oct 2026 (updated with your answers). Built on the brand sprint report and the four inspiration images.*

> **Your idea, made real. Even if it's just one mug.**

---

## 1. What the website has to do

The site has one main job: get someone with an idea (even a rough one) to send it in. Everything else supports that.

1. **Make starting feel easy.** A big, friendly "Start your order" button on every page, with an upload that accepts sketches, photos and screenshots, not just print-ready files.
2. **Prove the quality.** Real photos of finished work, close-ups of the print, and short stories from customers.
3. **Say "small orders welcome" loudly and honestly.** No minimum on any product: one piece is always fine.
4. **Show the design help.** This is the edge from the brand report. Most printers only print; EzyPrint helps you make it.

**Who visits:** a couple planning wedding favors, someone making a birthday shirt, a small business ordering its first branded mugs or stickers.

## 2. Look and feel

### What I took from your inspiration images

| Image | What to borrow | What to leave |
|---|---|---|
| **Natura** | Soft dusty blue, lots of white space, calm grid of products, clean logo on everything | The very formal serif; it's a bit too quiet for a playful brand |
| **Coastal Leads** | Deep navy with a warm yellow wave accent, friendly rounded sans-serif, products styled together as a "kit" | The water bottles (no flasks, as you said) |
| **"Your Logo" set** | Showing one logo across many items, which sells the idea of a branded set | The teal colour; we're going blue |
| **Illustrated shirt** | Fun, illustrated, character-style designs; back-print placement | All NASA branding removed. We keep only the playful illustration style |

### Colour palette (blue accent)

| Role | Name | Hex | Where it's used |
|---|---|---|---|
| Main brand colour | **Ink Navy** | `#1F3A68` | Headings, footer, main buttons |
| Accent | **Print Blue** | `#3F7CB5` | Links, icons, highlights, hover states |
| Soft background | **Sky Wash** | `#E5EEF7` | Section backgrounds, product tiles (like Natura) |
| Page background | **Paper** | `#FAF8F4` | Warm off-white so it never feels cold or corporate |
| Spark (use sparingly) | **Sunny Ink** | `#F2B43C` | Small touches only: a wave, a badge, "Small orders welcome" sticker. Taken from Coastal Leads |
| Text | **Charcoal** | `#1F2430` | Body text |

Blue carries trust and calm (the "Made properly" side). The small yellow spark carries the playful, warm side without getting loud, which matches "lively but subtle" from your report.

### Font pairing (chosen)

**Poppins (headings) + Nunito Sans (body).** Both are free Google Fonts.
- Poppins is a round, geometric sans, very close to the Coastal Leads look. Friendly and confident without shouting.
- Nunito Sans is soft and easy to read at small sizes, good for product details and the order form.

### Visual style
- Product photos on soft blue or warm paper backgrounds, in a tidy grid (Natura style).
- "Kit" photos: one design across a mug, shirt, tote and cap together (Coastal style). Great for weddings and small businesses.
- A gentle wave shape (navy + a thin yellow line) as a repeating brand element: section dividers, footer, packaging.
- Rounded corners, generous spacing, no harsh shadows.

### Page by page

**Home**
- Hero: big friendly line ("Got an idea? Let's print it.") over a kit photo, with "Start your order" and "See our work" buttons.
- Three promises from the brand report: *Bring any idea · Small orders welcome · Made with pride.*
- Product category tiles (soft blue grid).
- "How it works" in 3 steps.
- Weddings & events strip with a photo.
- Customer reviews.
- Instagram feed preview, with Instagram and Facebook links.
- Footer on every page: Instagram, Facebook, Messenger and WhatsApp icons, shop address and opening hours.

**Product category pages**
- Photo, short friendly description, colours/sizes, print options, price, turnaround time, and a "No minimum, even just one" badge.
- Two buttons: **Add to cart** (upload your ready design and buy) and **Need help with the design?** (opens the custom order form with this product filled in).

**Weddings & Events**
- Example packs (e.g. 50 mugs + 50 stickers + thank-you tags), photo stories, timeline advice ("order 3 weeks before your date").

**For Small Businesses**
- Starter kits (shirts for staff, branded mugs, logo stickers), reorders made easy, "we can help with your logo too".

**How It Works**
1. Send your idea (sketch, photo, or file).
2. We design it with you and send a preview to approve.
3. We print it properly, and you pick it up at the shop or we deliver it.

**Our Work (gallery)**
- Filterable by product and occasion. Short caption on each: who it was for and what they asked for.

**About**
- Your story: why you started EzyPrint, the "every order counts" belief, photos of you and the workspace.

**FAQ**
- File types, "I only have a sketch", minimums, pricing, turnaround, delivery, payment, changes after approval.

**Custom Order (for design help)**
- Product, quantity, rough deadline, upload (any image), a free-text "tell us your idea" box, contact details, preferred contact (Messenger, WhatsApp, phone or email).
- Friendly confirmation: "Got it! We'll get back to you within [X hours] with a preview and a price."

- Prices in pesos (₱).
- Payments: GCash, Maya, cards, and cash on pick-up.
- Delivery: same-day courier within Metro Manila (Lalamove or Grab), and J&T or LBC for provinces. Pick-up at the Quezon City shop.
- Contact section: Google Map of the shop, address, opening hours.

### Shopee, Lazada and TikTok Shop

Recommended setup: the website is your home base, and the marketplaces are extra doors into the same shop.
- The website shows "Also on Shopee, Lazada and TikTok Shop" buttons in the header, footer and product pages.
- Custom orders and design help stay on the website and Messenger/WhatsApp, because marketplaces handle ready-made listings best.
- Built on Shopify, TikTok Shop can sync products and orders through its official app, and Shopee and Lazada can sync through third-party apps. That syncing can wait until sales pick up; links are enough to start. (I'll confirm current app options and costs before building.)

### How ordering works

You said online and shop, so the plan is to offer both (tell me if you meant something else):
- **Online shop:** customers pick a product, upload a ready design, pay, and choose delivery or pick-up.
- **Custom orders:** customers who need design help send their idea, you reply with a preview and a price, then they pay.
- **Walk-in:** your physical shop address, map and hours are on the Contact section and in the footer.

## 6. Things I suggest adding

1. **Design preview step.** Customers approve a mockup before anything prints. It matches "Straight talk" and reduces reprints.
2. **"From" prices on every product.** Honest pricing up front builds trust and cuts back-and-forth messages.
3. **Chat buttons** for Messenger and WhatsApp floating on every page, and Instagram + Facebook links in the header and footer.
4. **Ready-made packs** for weddings, birthdays and small businesses. Easier to choose than building from scratch.
5. **Reviews with photos.** Ask every happy customer for a photo of their piece.
6. **Reorder link** for businesses: "Order the same again" without re-explaining.
7. **Later, not now:** an online mockup tool where customers place their design on a shirt or mug themselves.
